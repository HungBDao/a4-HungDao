DUAL DASH

A two-cube rhythm runner. Every spike lands on the beat of the song.

Goal

  • The top cube runs right, the bottom cube runs left. Jump every spike in both lanes until the song ends.
  • Spikes land on the beat, so listen as well as watch.

Controls

  • F jumps the top cube, J jumps the bottom cube.
  • Touch / mouse: tap the upper or lower part of the screen.
  • Hold to keep jumping. After a crash, press any key or tap to retry.

Settings (top-left panel)

  • Scroll speed: how early you see spikes coming (timing stays on the beat).
  • Jump height: higher jumps also last longer.
  • Audio offset: spikes feel early vs. the music → move right; late → move left.
  • Volume: music and sound effects.
